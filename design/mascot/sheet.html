Soteria · identity · round two — final

Bo

A gopher who keeps things in his cheeks. He is the face of Soteria — a Go desktop client that talks WebDAV to a server you run yourself — and he does two jobs: at 200 px he is a character, at 16 px he is a folder with ears.

The name is the b in Soteria. It is also, in Vietnamese, ôm bo bo — to hold onto something and refuse to let go. That is the whole product in two syllables.

01

Construction

Built on a 12 × 12 grid of 20 px units. The head and the pouches are one outline, not a head with two circles stuck to it — the pouches inflate the silhouette, and that changing silhouette is the whole recognition cue. Everything is one weight: 3 units at 240, dropping to 2.4 for interior lines, so he sits beside the app's 1.6-stroke icon set without shouting.

Rules of the drawing

  • ✓Flat fills, one ink outline, no gradients and no shadow — same discipline as the UI.
  • ✓Almond eyes, never white ovals. Pupil only, with one highlight up and to the right.
  • ✓One stroke for the mouth, corners below the middle. Bo sulks; he never grins, and he has no teeth.
  • ✕Ears stay small and sit on the side of the skull. Big round ears on top make him a bear.
  • ✕No extra colours on the fur. If Bo needs to say something, the thing he holds says it.
  • ✕Never rotate or shear him. He stands square; the props move.
02

Five states, five screens

The pouches are the transfer queue, so Bo is not decoration — he is a status indicator you can read across a room. Each pose is tied to a real state the app already emits.

Idle

Files, connected

Pouches at rest. The default Bo.

Carrying

Upload / download running

Pouches loaded, one more in his arms.

Stuffed

Queue backed up

Four in the cheeks. Comic, not alarming.

Empty-handed

Empty folder

Deflated pouches, a folder with nothing in it.

Asleep

Offline / reconnecting

Eyes shut until Ping() comes back.

03

The mark

The app's own left-tab folder with Bo behind it. The face sits on the front pocket and the ears peek over its top edge — that edge runs straight across, so the ears stay symmetric even though the tab does not. Nothing here is invented: it is the folder from the file list, at icon scale. Below 24 px it swaps to a reduced cut with the muzzle and the highlights removed and the eyes carrying the whole face.

04

Lockups

Geist 600 at −1.8 tracking, set in ink. Clear space on every side is one ear — the radius of the ear circle at whatever size the mark is drawn. The wordmark is always lowercase.

05

Palette

Bo does not theme. Five constant colours for the animal himself, and everything he carries — file cards, the empty folder, the sleep marks — is drawn in the app's own --folder-* tokens rather than a colour of its own. In dark mode the app goes dark around him and he stays the same animal.

06

What ships

All ten files are plain SVG in design/mascot/, generated from one source — build.mjs — so the character has a single definition. Edit the script, re-run node design/mascot/build.mjs, and every pose, the mark and the icon change together.
